# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

The **Omoji** community — maintained by [Aeroclipse Proprietary Limited](https://aeroclipse-bw.web.app) — is committed to being a welcoming and respectful space for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, gender identity, nationality, race, religion, or any other characteristic.

We hold ourselves and each other to a high standard. Contributors who uphold this standard are the ones we want to work with long-term.

---

## Our Standards

**Behaviour we expect:**

- Respectful, constructive communication at all times
- Welcoming feedback — giving and receiving it gracefully
- Focusing on what moves the project and community forward
- Empathy towards fellow contributors and users

**Behaviour that will not be tolerated:**

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks of any kind
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent
- Deliberate disruption of community spaces or discussions
- Any conduct unbecoming of a professional environment

---

## Enforcement

Violations are handled by the Aeroclipse team. Depending on severity, consequences range from a private warning to a permanent ban from the project community.

All decisions are final and rest with Aeroclipse Proprietary Limited.

---

## Reporting

If you witness or experience a violation:

**🌐 [aeroclipse-bw.web.app](https://aeroclipse-bw.web.app)**

Reports are handled confidentially. We aim to respond within **72 hours**.
